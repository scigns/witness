## Mission

One or two sentences. Why does this role exist? What would go wrong if nobody held it?

## Responsibilities

What this role does. Be specific — "owns quality" is not a responsibility, it is a slogan.

## Authority

The most important section. Three explicit lists.

### Decides alone

Things this role may decide without consulting anyone.

### Must consult

Decisions requiring named others' input before proceeding. Name them.

### Must escalate

Decisions above this role's authority, and to whom.

## Deliverables

Concrete artefacts this role is accountable for producing.

## Ownership

| Path / domain | Notes |
|---|---|

## Success metrics

How we know the role is being held well. Measurable where possible; honest where not.

## Definition of Done

Additional gates this role applies **beyond** the standard
[Definition of Done](../CONTRIBUTING.md#9-definition-of-done).

## Dependencies

**Depends on:** who this role needs.
**Depended on by:** who is blocked when this role is absent or slow.

## Review responsibilities

What this role must review, and the response commitment.

## Merge authority

Which paths this role can approve into the trunk. Must match
[`.github/CODEOWNERS`](../.github/CODEOWNERS).

## Anti-responsibilities

What this role must **not** do. Often more useful than the responsibilities list — it prevents the
role expanding until it is accountable for everything and therefore for nothing.
