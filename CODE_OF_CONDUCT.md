# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and leaders pledge to make participation in the Witness community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion,
Indigenous status, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive
and healthy community.

## Why this matters more here than usual

Witness handles the recorded voices of people who are often not in the room where software gets
built: community members giving testimony, Indigenous knowledge custodians, people speaking to
government about things that affect their lives.

A community that cannot treat its own contributors with respect will not build software that
treats those people with respect. The conduct standard and the product standard are the same
standard.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and learning
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community and for
  the people who will eventually be recorded by this software
- **Deferring to lived expertise.** When someone with direct experience of a context — Indigenous
  data governance, disability and assistive technology, operating systems in a low-resource
  ministry, being on the receiving end of a consultation — tells you your design is wrong, start
  from the assumption that they are right.
- **Being direct and being kind.** These are not in tension. Vague feedback to spare feelings
  wastes people's time; harsh feedback to seem rigorous wastes people's goodwill.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without explicit
  permission
- Dismissing accessibility, consent, sovereignty or Indigenous data governance concerns as
  "overhead", "politics" or "not engineering" — they are core requirements of this project
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or relitigating settled decisions without new evidence
- Advocating for or encouraging use of Witness for surveillance, or for processing without consent
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable
behaviour and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behaviour that
they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit or reject comments, commits,
code, wiki edits, issues and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and
will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions, chat, mailing lists, calls — and also applies when an individual is officially
representing the community in public spaces, including at conferences and in deployments where
they are identified as a Witness contributor.

## Reporting

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to the Code of Conduct committee at
the contacts listed in [`docs/governance/CONDUCT_CONTACTS.md`](docs/governance/CONDUCT_CONTACTS.md).

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. All community leaders are
obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

**If your report concerns a member of the Code of Conduct committee or project leadership**, you
may report to any other committee member directly, or to the neutral external contact listed in
the same document. Reports about leadership are handled by members who are not the subject of the
report, and the subject is recused from all deliberation.

### What to expect

1. **Acknowledgement within 2 working days.**
2. **A named handler** who will stay with your report through to resolution.
3. **Investigation** — we will speak with those involved, and will not share your identity beyond
   what is necessary, without your agreement.
4. **A decision and the reasoning**, communicated to you.
5. **Follow-up** to confirm the situation is resolved.

You may withdraw a report at any time. We will not retaliate, and we will not tolerate retaliation
by others — retaliation is itself a serious violation.

## Enforcement guidelines

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining the consequences
for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct:

### 1. Correction

**Community impact:** Use of inappropriate language or other behaviour deemed unprofessional or
unwelcome.
**Consequence:** A private, written warning from community leaders, providing clarity around the
nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behaviour was inappropriate. A public
apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Community impact:** A violation through a single incident or series of actions.
**Consequence:** A warning with consequences for continued behaviour. No interaction with the
people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a
specified period. This includes avoiding interactions in community spaces as well as external
channels. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary ban

**Community impact:** A serious violation of community standards, including sustained
inappropriate behaviour.
**Consequence:** A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the
community for a specified period. No public or private interaction with the people involved,
including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, is allowed during this
period. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent ban

**Community impact:** Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, including
sustained inappropriate behaviour, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or
disparagement of classes of individuals.
**Consequence:** A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the community.

## Appeals

Any enforcement decision may be appealed once, in writing, within 30 days, to the Steering
Committee (see [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md)). The appeal is heard by members who were not
involved in the original decision. The Steering Committee's decision on appeal is final.
