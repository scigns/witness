# Code of Conduct Contacts

**Owner:** Open Source Lead (process) · Code of Conduct committee (decisions)
**Status:** Active

## Reporting

*Named committee members and a reporting address will be published here as the committee is
constituted. Until then, reports may be sent privately to any Steering Committee member.*

We publish this gap rather than a placeholder address, because a reporting channel nobody monitors is
worse than an honest statement of where we are.

## Reporting about leadership

**If your report concerns a member of the Code of Conduct committee, the CTO or the Founder**, you may
report to any other committee member directly, or to the neutral external contact that will be named
here.

Reports about leadership are handled by members who are not the subject of the report, and the subject
is **recused from all deliberation**. This is not a courtesy — a process where the powerful adjudicate
complaints about themselves is not a process.

## What to expect

1. Acknowledgement within **2 working days**
2. A **named handler** who stays with your report through to resolution
3. Investigation — we will not share your identity beyond what is necessary without your agreement
4. A decision, with the reasoning, communicated to you
5. Follow-up to confirm resolution

You may withdraw a report at any time. **We will not retaliate and will not tolerate retaliation** —
retaliation is itself a serious violation.
