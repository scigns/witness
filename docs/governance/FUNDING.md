# Funding

**Owner:** Founder & Steering Committee
**Status:** Active
**Disclosure rule:** every funding source above the materiality threshold is published here, **with
the conditions attached to it**.

---

## Why we publish conditions, not just amounts

An amount tells you very little. What determines whether funding compromises a project is what the
funder can ask for in return. So this page records conditions, not just sums — and if a funder is
unwilling to have their conditions published, that is itself disqualifying.

## Current funding

*None recorded. Witness is at its foundation stage.*

## Acceptable funding

- Public-sector grants
- Philanthropic funding
- Development-partner programme funding
- Paid support, training, integration and deployment services from **any** vendor
- Dedicated staff time contributed by adopting institutions

## Unacceptable funding

Any funding conditioned on:

- Closing the source, or creating a proprietary edition
- Making capability available only through a hosted offering
- **Weakening the sovereignty default** — including "just opt-in telemetry"
- Data access for the funder
- Preferential feature control not available to other contributors
- Restricting who may adopt or fork Witness

This list is binding, not aspirational. Rejecting funding on these grounds requires no further
approval; **accepting** funding that touches any of them requires unanimous Steering Committee
approval and would, in practice, be vetoed by the Governance Lead.

## Commercial services

**Encouraged.** We want a healthy ecosystem of vendors deploying and supporting Witness — that is how
infrastructure becomes sustainable, and how institutions get support in their own time zone and
language.

What we will not do is privilege one of them, **including any organisation the founders are involved
in**. Conflicts of interest are declared and members recuse themselves.

## Transparency

- Funding above the materiality threshold is disclosed here within 30 days of agreement
- Conditions attached are disclosed in full
- Steering Committee members declare conflicts and recuse accordingly
- Minutes are public unless they concern an individual
