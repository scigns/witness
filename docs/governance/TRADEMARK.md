# Trademark and Naming Policy

**Owner:** Founder (Stage 1) → Foundation (Stage 3)
**Status:** Active

---

## Position

The Witness name and marks are held by the Founder during governance Stage 1 and will transfer to a
neutral foundation at Stage 3 ([`GOVERNANCE.md`](../../GOVERNANCE.md)).

The purpose of holding the mark is narrow: so that "Witness" continues to mean something specific to
an institution evaluating it. It is **not** a mechanism for controlling use, restricting forks, or
retaining leverage after the code has been released.

## What you may do without asking

- **Use** Witness, for anything, commercially or otherwise
- **Modify** it and run your modified version
- **Fork** it, for any reason including disagreement with our governance
- Say that you **support, deploy, host, integrate with, or are compatible with** Witness
- Describe your service as "Witness hosting", "Witness support", "Witness integration"
- Use the name to refer to the project accurately, including in comparisons and criticism
- Teach, write about, or present on Witness

## What requires agreement

- Implying **official endorsement**, certification or partnership
- Distributing a **modified version under the Witness name** — call it something else, or make the
  distinction clear
- Using the marks in **your** product name, company name or domain in a way that suggests we produced it
- Using our logo as your logo

## The distinction

The line is **accuracy about who made what**. An institution should always be able to tell whether it
is running the project's software or someone's derivative, and whether a vendor's endorsement claim is
real. That is the whole of the policy.

We will not use trademark to suppress forks, criticism, competition or unfavourable comparison. A
project that claims the right to fork as a feature ([`DIGITAL_SOVEREIGNTY.md`](DIGITAL_SOVEREIGNTY.md))
and then uses trademark to make forking impractical would be arguing in bad faith.

## If in doubt

Ask. We expect to say yes to almost everything.
