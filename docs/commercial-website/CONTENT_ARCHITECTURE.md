# Witness Commercial Website Content Architecture

**Owner:** Product and Content
**Status:** Initial planning baseline
**Last reviewed:** 2026-09-02

## Purpose

Organise public content around the visitor journey: discover, understand, believe, trust and act.
Routes are created only when useful reviewed content exists.

## Initial release sequence

1. `/` — promise, problem, method, audiences, differentiation, trust preview and next action.
2. `/platform`, `/how-it-works`, `/why-witness` — product explanation before application entry.
3. Four priority solution pages — government, international development, research and consultation.
4. Reconciled `/pricing`, `/contact` and later `/demo/request` and `/start`.
5. Synthetic `/demo`, trust centre, resources, legitimate customer evidence and documentation.

## Navigation contract

Target labels are Platform, Solutions, Customers, Resources, Pricing and Trust, with Sign in and a
single context-appropriate conversion CTA. Do not link unavailable destinations.

## Content truth rules

- Lead with the institutional problem and outcome, not implementation features.
- Explain provenance through real mental models and synthetic data only.
- Reconcile all pricing, deployment and security language with implemented and operational evidence.
- Label planned capabilities. Never use placeholder pages to imply availability.
- Customer, legal and certification content remains human-gated.

## Reusable source material

Canonical product language comes from `VISION.md`. Product implementation and trust evidence comes
from accepted ADRs, architecture, code, tests and operational proof. Sector material under
`docs/product/SECTOR_APPLICATIONS.md` is explicitly contextual and requires content review before use.
Commercial operating documents are inputs, not automatically publishable copy.
