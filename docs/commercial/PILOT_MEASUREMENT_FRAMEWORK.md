# Pilot measurement framework

**Owner:** Product Director & Institutional Customer Success Lead
**Status:** Controlled-pilot methodology
**Review:** At pilot baseline, midpoint and evaluation

Witness measures governance value, not engagement theatre. Do not fabricate ROI or treat login
counts as success. Measurement is local by default and must work without third-party analytics or
external telemetry.

## Before/after questions

- Why was this decision made?
- Who participated?
- Who approved it?
- Where did this requirement originate?
- What evidence informed it?
- Did consultation feedback change implementation?

For each question capture baseline retrieval time, Witness retrieval time, systems searched, human
effort, completeness, provenance, confidence and limitations. Use comparable synthetic or approved
real scenarios and record the method.

## Product evidence

Where technically and ethically appropriate, measure organisations onboarded, active programmes,
sessions captured, decisions recorded, approver/provenance coverage, evidence links, actions
resolved, exports, audit verifications, unresolved decisions and time to retrieve history.
Server-derived counts remain operational evidence; they are not automatically customer value.

## Interpretation

Report sample size, missing data, workflow changes, facilitator support and confounders. A faster
answer with incomplete provenance is not success. A small pilot supports a learning claim, not a
general market or ROI claim.
