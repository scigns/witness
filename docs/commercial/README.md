# Commercial programme

**Owner:** Product Director & Commercial Lead
**Status:** Controlled Institutional Pilot programme
**Review:** Monthly during the first 90 days, then quarterly

This directory supports Witness's first paid and first renewing institutional customer. It is a
commercial operating pack, not legal, tax, accounting or procurement advice. Every customer-facing
artefact requires authorised human review before use. Store executed contracts, customer data,
bank details and confidential diligence evidence outside the public repository.

Witness is **governance evidence infrastructure**, not AI meeting summarisation software:

> Institutional memory you can prove.

## Two revenue gates

### Revenue Gate A — externally administered controlled pilot

Witness may be sold before C3 when an appropriate supplier exists; scope is agreed; human legal and
tax review occurs; quotation, invoice and payment administration happen outside Witness; funds go
to the supplier's business bank account; and provisioning follows the controlled-pilot process.
Witness must not claim it generated the invoice, processed or reconciled payment, or automatically
activated paid entitlements.

### Revenue Gate B — Witness-native institutional lifecycle

C3 must prove `invoice → settlement → reconciliation → subscription transition → entitlement
activation` with tenant isolation, auditability, stale-intent protection and exactly-once behavior.

## Operating principles

- Paid pilots are the default; free pilots require an explicit strategic reason and approval.
- Configuration comes before customisation.
- One core product serves all archetypes through plans, configuration, deployment and agreements.
- No third-party analytics by default; sovereign deployments measure value locally.
- Documented controls are not represented as implemented controls.
- C4 provider automation is demand-gated by real customer evidence.
