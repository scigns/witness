# Product positioning

**Owner:** Product Director
**Status:** Commercial hypothesis for validation
**Review:** After each qualified prospect and pilot evaluation

## Positioning

> **WITNESS — Institutional memory you can prove.**

Governance infrastructure for organisations that need to prove not only what they decided, but how
they arrived there.

## Problem

Decisions are fragmented across minutes, email, documents and individual memory. Institutions often
cannot efficiently show who participated, what evidence was considered, why a choice was made, or
whether consultation changed implementation.

## Differentiation

Witness combines consent, provenance, human confirmation, participatory-governance evidence,
Indigenous/data-sovereignty principles, auditable history and sovereign deployment. These are core
product characteristics, not compliance decoration.

## Language controls

Use `Controlled Institutional Pilot` or `Commercial Pilot`. Do not claim GA, certification,
compliance, guaranteed ROI, authoritative AI output, or a payment capability that does not exist.
Do not position Witness as a generic meeting summariser, document repository or autonomous agent.
