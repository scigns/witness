# Design partner programme

**Owner:** Product Director & Institutional Customer Success Lead
**Status:** Configurable programme hypothesis
**Review:** Per pilot and at each 90-day board checkpoint

## Purpose

Recruit institutions with a real governance-evidence problem, a sponsor, an operational owner and a
willingness to measure value. Design partners receive a bounded controlled pilot, not unrestricted
product customisation.

## Lifecycle

`DISCOVERY → PROPOSAL → PROCUREMENT → IMPLEMENTATION → ONBOARDING → PILOT → EVALUATION → RENEWAL DECISION`

## Entry criteria

- identifiable governance question and decision workflow;
- sponsor and operational owner;
- acceptable consent, sovereignty and security posture;
- measurable baseline and evaluation window;
- procurement route and budget hypothesis;
- request classification and go/no-go review completed.

## Commercial posture

Paid is the default. A free pilot requires a recorded strategic reason, bounded scope, equivalent
evaluation commitments and approval by the Product Director and Commercial Lead. Free work must not
hide unwillingness to pay or become indefinite consulting.

## Partner obligations

Nominate authorised participants, review consent/data handling, attend onboarding, provide baseline
and evaluation evidence, report incidents promptly and participate in the renewal decision. No case
study or public naming is implied.
