# Witness product overview

**Owner:** Product Director
**Status:** Controlled Institutional Pilot
**Review:** Before every external use

Witness is governance evidence infrastructure for organisations that need to prove not only what
they decided, but how they arrived there. It helps authorised people reconstruct participation,
approval, evidence, alternatives, consultation influence and subsequent action without relying on
one employee's memory.

## Current controlled-pilot capability

- organisation and programme-scoped access;
- sessions, participants and consent history;
- provenance-backed evidence and human review;
- decisions, commitments and actions;
- summaries, approved reports and redacted exports;
- hash-chained audit history;
- local transcription and AI-assisted drafts subject to human confirmation;
- sovereign, hybrid and development deployment profiles;
- plan catalogue, subscriptions, entitlements and non-activating commercial intent.

## Product boundaries

Witness does not make decisions, determine lawful basis, provide legal/tax advice, certify
compliance, or make machine output institutional truth. Before C3, it also does not generate
invoices, process or reconcile payments, or activate paid entitlements from settlement.

## One product, configurable contexts

| Archetype | Core workflow | Configuration/deployment emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| Regional programme governance | Meetings → evidence → decisions → actions → reports | Multiple programmes/communities, low bandwidth, consent configuration |
| Participatory co-design / education | Workshops → contributions → review → influence evidence | Participant provenance, dissent, consent and feedback incorporation |
| Government digital transformation | Requirements → workshops → approvals → change history | Formal roles, dedicated/sovereign deployment, procurement and audit |

These are synthetic archetypes. They are not customer claims or separate applications.
