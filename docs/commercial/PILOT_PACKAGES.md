# Commercial pilot packages

**Owner:** Commercial Lead & Product Director
**Status:** Negotiation and validation hypotheses
**Review:** After every proposal and quarterly

These ranges are indicative AUD hypotheses, not product-catalogue prices, quotations, offers, tax
advice or fixed legal terms. Every proposal must separately review scope, currency, tax and supplier
authority.

| Package | Indicative hypothesis | Intended context |
|---|---:|---|
| Design Partner Pilot | AUD 5,000–10,000 | Bounded team/programme pilot with high learning value |
| Institutional Pilot | AUD 12,000–25,000 | Formal organisation pilot with onboarding, training and support |
| Complex Government / Enterprise Pilot | AUD 20,000–40,000+ | Dedicated/sovereign deployment, security/procurement effort or integrations |

## Required proposal decomposition

Every package shows separately:

- software licence;
- implementation;
- onboarding;
- training;
- support;
- hosting;
- dedicated deployment;
- integration or approved custom work.

A component may be zero or included, but must not be hidden. This prevents implementation revenue
from masking weak recurring software value and makes renewal economics reviewable.

## Archetype configuration

The three programme archetypes use the same Witness build. Differences belong in institutional
profile, consent template, roles, workspace/session setup, deployment profile, support plan and
commercial agreement—not forked code.
