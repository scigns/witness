# 90-day commercialisation dashboard

**Owner:** CTO & Product Director
**Status:** Approved planning horizon; actuals not yet recorded
**Review:** Weekly operating review and monthly board review

Do not convert these milestones into unsupported calendar promises. Update actual state, blockers,
evidence and next action from observed facts.

| Milestone | Owner role | Target state within 90 days | Actual state | Blockers | Evidence | Next action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| First qualified prospect | Commercial Lead | Sponsor, use case, budget/procurement and fit qualified | Not started | No active opportunity recorded | Approved qualification framework | Begin targeted discovery |
| First proposal | Commercial Lead | Reviewed paid-pilot proposal issued | Not started | Enablement pack requires approval | Proposal templates | Validate package with prospect |
| First paid pilot | Commercial Lead | Gate A payment or Gate B payment legitimately received | Not started | Supplier/customer/procurement evidence required | External or C3 payment evidence | Pursue Gate A in parallel with C3 |
| C3 technically complete | Backend Lead | Gate B lifecycle proven exactly once | Not started | Issues 4–10 | Tests, migrations, audit and runbooks | Start only after programme PR approval |
| First controlled production pilot | Product Director | Go/no-go approved; real-data gates evidenced | Not started | Customer and deployment-specific gates | Signed decision and rehearsal | Qualify design partner |
| First evaluation | Customer Success Lead | Baseline/after evaluation completed | Not started | Pilot not selected | Evaluation evidence | Agree measures during discovery |
| First renewal decision | Customer Sponsor & Commercial Lead | Explicit renewal outcome recorded | Not started | Requires completed paid pilot | Renewal review | Build evaluation into proposal |

## Parallel programme cadence

- Weeks 1–4: enablement, prospect discovery; C3 domain/persistence design.
- Weeks 5–8: proposal/procurement; invoice issuance and manual reconciliation.
- Weeks 9–12: pilot readiness/value baseline; exactly-once, isolation and recovery proof.
- Week 13: board go/no-go, release evidence and next-quarter decision.

C4 is not a default next milestone. Assess payment-method demand after C3 and real procurement
evidence.
