# Pricing assumptions

**Owner:** Commercial Lead & SaaS Revenue Operations Lead
**Status:** Hypotheses under validation
**Review:** Monthly during active selling

Hosted FREE, TEAM and ORGANISATION prices come from the authoritative `Plan` / `PlanPrice`
catalogue and must not be duplicated or silently changed here. INSTITUTIONAL remains quote-based.

## Hypotheses to test

- buyers pay for governance evidence, deployment confidence and implementation—not summary volume;
- paid pilots reduce ambiguous commitment and improve sponsor participation;
- institutional buyers prefer invoice, PO and bank transfer initially;
- annual contract value may combine recurring licence/support with non-recurring implementation;
- willingness to pay differs by procurement burden and deployment topology.

## Evidence record

For each qualified opportunity record range tested, package, objections, procurement duration,
accepted/declined outcome, recurring/non-recurring split and confidence. Store customer-identifying
records outside the public repository.

Do not infer pricing validity from one buyer, confuse consulting revenue with recurring value, or
encode revenue targets into entitlements.
