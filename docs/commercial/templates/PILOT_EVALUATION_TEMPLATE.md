# Pilot evaluation template

**Owner:** Product Director & Institutional Customer Success Lead
**Status:** Reusable evidence template

## Context and method

Pilot scope, sample, support provided, baseline method, deviations and limitations.

## Before/after evidence

Compare retrieval time, systems searched, human effort, completeness, provenance and confidence for
the same approved questions. Include sessions, decisions, evidence links, approver/provenance
coverage, actions, exports, incidents and unresolved governance decisions where appropriate.

## Findings

State observed value, failures, consent/security concerns, support burden, implementation burden and
what cannot be concluded. Do not fabricate ROI or generalise from an inadequate sample.

## Recommendation

`RENEW`, `EXPAND`, `CONTINUE CONDITIONALLY`, `PAUSE`, or `END`, with evidence and conditions.
