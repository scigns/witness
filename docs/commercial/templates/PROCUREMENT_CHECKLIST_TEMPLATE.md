# Procurement checklist template

**Owner:** Enterprise Procurement Adviser & Commercial Lead
**Status:** Template; not procurement or legal advice

- [ ] Customer and supplier legal entities verified by authorised humans
- [ ] Buyer, sponsor and budget authority identified
- [ ] Vendor onboarding and procurement thresholds understood
- [ ] Package components and request classifications clear
- [ ] Security, privacy, consent and data-residency reviews routed
- [ ] Deployment and support responsibilities agreed
- [ ] PO/customer-reference requirement confirmed
- [ ] Currency, invoice fields and tax treatment professionally reviewed
- [ ] Revenue Gate A or B explicitly selected
- [ ] Payment goes only to approved supplier business account
- [ ] No bank credential/card data enters Witness or this document
- [ ] Acceptance and authorised signatories confirmed outside Witness
- [ ] Renewal/evaluation process included
