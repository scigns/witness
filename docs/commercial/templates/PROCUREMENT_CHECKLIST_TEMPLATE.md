# Procurement checklist template

**Owner:** Enterprise Procurement Adviser & Commercial Lead
**Status:** Template; not procurement or legal advice

- [ ] Customer and supplier legal entities verified by authorised humans
- [ ] Buyer, sponsor and budget authority identified
- [ ] Vendor onboarding and procurement thresholds understood
- [ ] Package components and request classifications clear
- [ ] Security, privacy, consent and data-residency reviews routed
- [ ] Deployment and support responsibilities agreed
- [ ] PO/customer-reference requirement confirmed
- [ ] Currency, invoice fields and tax treatment professionally reviewed
- [ ] No bank credential/card data enters Witness or this document
- [ ] Acceptance and authorised signatories confirmed outside Witness
- [ ] Renewal/evaluation process included

## Revenue Gate A — external administration

- [ ] Gate A explicitly selected
- [ ] Quotation, invoice and payment administration occur in approved external systems
- [ ] Payment goes only to the approved supplier business bank account
- [ ] Witness makes no claim that it generated, processed or reconciled the payment or automatically
      activated entitlements

## Revenue Gate B — Witness-native C3

Gate B is `UNAVAILABLE` unless every item below has linked, accepted evidence:

- [ ] Gate B explicitly selected
- [ ] C3 release and version evidence
- [ ] Invoice capability evidence
- [ ] Settlement and reconciliation evidence
- [ ] Exactly-once settlement-to-entitlement evidence
- [ ] Commercial tenant-isolation evidence
- [ ] Relevant release and security approval

Gate B does not make Witness a bank or permit bank credentials to enter Witness.
