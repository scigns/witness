**Owner:** Commercial Lead & Institutional Customer Success Lead
**Status:** Reusable decision template

- Contract/pilot reference and review period: `[REQUIRED]`
- Revenue Gate: `[A/B — REQUIRED]`
- Payment status: `[REQUIRED]`
- Payment/settlement evidence: `[REQUIRED]`
- Original paid pilot commercial evidence: `[REQUIRED]`
- Sponsor and operational-user assessment: `[REQUIRED]`
- Governance-value evidence and limitations: `[REQUIRED]`
- Security, consent and incident position: `[REQUIRED]`
- Support/implementation effort: `[REQUIRED]`
- Recurring versus non-recurring revenue: `[REQUIRED]`
- Revenue, attributable cost and margin evidence where available: `[REQUIRED OR NOT AVAILABLE]`
- Outstanding conditions and request classifications: `[REQUIRED]`
- Data retention/export/exit requirements: `[REQUIRED]`
- Decision: `RENEW`, `EXPAND`, `CONTINUE CONDITIONALLY`, `PAUSE`, or `END`
- Rationale, approver roles and next action: `[REQUIRED]`

For Revenue Gate A, reference approved external commercial and payment evidence without copying
confidential bank or customer information into the public repository. For Revenue Gate B, reference
Witness-native settlement evidence. Gate B is not required for a Gate A customer to renew.

A renewal decision must not be completed while any mandatory evidence is blank. `PAUSE` and `END`
remain valid renewal decisions. A **successful paid renewal** requires a previous paying customer to
complete an authorised paid `RENEW` or `EXPAND` outcome and provide evidence of continued service or
entitlement.
