# Pilot statement of work template

**Owner:** Product Director & Commercial Lead
**Status:** Template requiring professional review

## Parties and authority

`[LEGAL/PROCUREMENT REVIEW REQUIRED]`

## Problem, outcome and scope

Describe the governance problem, selected synthetic archetype, in-scope workflows, deliverables,
customer responsibilities, Witness responsibilities and explicit exclusions.

## Request classification

List configuration, integrations and separately approved custom work. No request automatically
becomes core product.

## Deployment and data

Record topology, residency, egress, identity, consent, retention, backup/restore and incident owner.
Do not include credentials or confidential operational values.

## Commercial and support

Reference the approved quotation, revenue gate, support configuration and professional-review
placeholders. Do not fabricate terms or SLAs.

## Success and exit

Define baseline, evaluation method, renewal decision date/window, permitted outcomes and data exit.
