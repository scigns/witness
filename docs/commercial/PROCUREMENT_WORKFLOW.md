# Institutional procurement workflow

**Owner:** Commercial Lead & Enterprise Procurement Adviser
**Status:** Controlled-pilot operating framework
**Review:** Per opportunity

## Revenue Gate A — external administration

1. Record quote interest without implying purchase.
2. Qualify scope, sponsor, supplier entity and procurement route.
3. Prepare an externally reviewed quotation/proposal.
4. Customer accepts terms outside Witness.
5. Supplier records PO/reference in its approved external process.
6. Supplier issues an external invoice.
7. Customer pays the supplier business bank account.
8. Supplier verifies payment outside Witness.
9. Witness operator provisions the controlled pilot under the approved go/no-go.
10. Retain evidence in authorised business systems, not this repository.

Witness must not claim it generated, processed or reconciled this invoice/payment, or automatically
activated entitlements.

## Revenue Gate B — Witness-native C3

C3 will own invoice truth, settlement evidence, reconciliation, subscription transition and
entitlement activation. Until that capability is implemented and accepted, Gate B is `UNAVAILABLE`.
Selection requires linked evidence for the C3 release/version, invoice capability,
settlement/reconciliation, exactly-once settlement-to-entitlement application, commercial tenant
isolation, and relevant release/security approval. Gate B records commercial truth and coordinates
authorised transitions; it does not make Witness a bank or payment processor.

## Procurement questions

Confirm customer entity, buyer authority, procurement thresholds, vendor onboarding, PO need,
security/privacy review, data residency, invoice fields, payment terms, currency, tax review,
insurance/certification requests, support expectations and approval sequence. Record unknowns as
conditions; never invent answers.
