# Controlled-pilot support model

**Owner:** Institutional Customer Success Lead & Platform Reliability Lead
**Status:** Configurable framework; no SLA commitment
**Review:** Per pilot and after every material incident

## Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Customer sponsor | Outcomes, internal authority and renewal decision |
| Customer operational owner | Users, scheduling, local process and escalation |
| Witness pilot owner | Scope, coordination and customer communication |
| Platform operator | Deployment, health, backup, restore and access operations |
| Security lead | Security/consent incident coordination |

## Support design

Each proposal configures support channel, coverage window, named escalation roles, planned-session
support, maintenance communication and manual fallback. These are planning fields, not guaranteed
response or resolution times. Any SLA requires separate authorised commercial and operational
review.

Use existing incident severity and consent-first controls. Add commercial reconciliation incident
handling before Revenue Gate B. Never put participant content, credentials, bank details or
customer-confidential evidence into public issues.
