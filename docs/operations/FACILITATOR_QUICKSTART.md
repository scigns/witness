# Witness Facilitator Quick Start

**For:** The person preparing and running a Witness session.

This is the short session guide.

You do not need to understand the technical infrastructure behind Witness.

Your job is to:

> prepare the session, make consent clear, capture what happened, review the record and confirm the
> outcomes.

---

## Before the session

### 1. Sign in

Open Witness and sign in with your own account.

Confirm:

- you are in the correct organisation;
- you can see the correct program;
- you can see or create the session you need.

If the program is missing, contact the Witness administrator rather than using another person's
account.

---

### 2. Prepare the session

Open the program and session.

Check:

- session title;
- purpose;
- agenda;
- participants;
- consent configuration;
- required resources;
- whether audio recording will be used;
- expected outputs.

Keep the session configuration as simple as possible.

---

### 3. Check participants and consent

Before capturing participant material:

- explain why the session is being captured;
- explain what will be recorded;
- explain how contributions may be used;
- respect attribution/anonymity choices;
- record consent where required.

Do not interpret the presence of a button in Witness as permission to use it.

Consent comes first.

---

### 4. Test the device

If audio recording is being used:

- check the microphone;
- confirm the browser can access it;
- make a short synthetic test if needed;
- know where Start, Pause, Resume and Stop are.

If the recording feature is unavailable, use the agreed fallback rather than changing browser
security settings during the session.

---

## During the session

### 5. Keep the correct session open

Before capturing anything, check that you are still in the intended session.

Avoid having several similar session tabs open at the same time.

---

### 6. Capture contributions

Depending on the session, you may capture:

- facilitator notes;
- participant contributions;
- audio;
- documents/images;
- discussion evidence;
- decisions;
- commitments;
- actions.

Associate participant material with the correct participant where required.

For participant-submitted documents/images, the relevant evidence-submission permission must be
present.

Do not put participant evidence into Resources merely to avoid the evidence workflow.

---

### 7. Recording

If recording:

- make sure participants know recording has started;
- pause when recording should not continue;
- stop when the relevant part of the session ends.

Do not leave recording running simply because the session is still open.

---

### 8. If something fails

If Witness becomes unavailable or connectivity is poor:

1. tell the session lead;
2. stop relying on the failing feature;
3. use the agreed manual note-taking fallback where appropriate;
4. continue obtaining consent independently of Witness;
5. do not repeatedly submit the same item if you are unsure whether it succeeded.

Do not upload fallback material later unless its source and consent basis remain clear.

For serious privacy, consent or data-isolation concerns, stop the affected Witness workflow and
contact the support owner.

---

## After the session

### 9. Check the evidence

Confirm:

- expected notes are present;
- expected attachments are present;
- failed items are identified;
- evidence belongs to the correct session.

Do this before moving on to reports.

---

### 10. Review transcripts

A transcript is a working draft until a human reviews it.

Correct important errors before relying on it.

For Fijian/iTaukei sessions:

> Automated transcription is not currently approved as an authoritative transcript.

Use original audio plus manual/human verification where accurate text is required.

---

### 11. Review the summary

Generated summaries are suggestions.

Review them against the session evidence.

Correct:

- factual errors;
- missing context;
- incorrect attribution;
- overstatement;
- decisions that were never actually made.

A machine-generated statement does not become institutional truth simply because Witness generated
it.

---

### 12. Confirm outcomes

Record the actual:

- decisions;
- commitments;
- actions.

Check owners and due dates where relevant.

Only record outcomes that were actually agreed or confirmed.

---

### 13. Review reports

Before publication/export:

- check the report content;
- check its audience;
- confirm human review is complete;
- confirm sensitive content is appropriate for the intended recipient;
- confirm provenance where needed.

Do not circulate a generated report without review.

---

### 14. Export

Use the appropriate available format for the intended recipient.

Witness may provide formats including:

- HTML;
- Markdown;
- JSON;
- CSV.

Choose the format based on how the client will use the information.

---

## At the end

### 15. Close the loop

Before leaving the session:

- confirm the record is complete enough;
- confirm major outcomes;
- note anything that failed or confused you;
- sign out when appropriate.

After the session, give operational/product feedback to the Witness pilot owner.

Do not include confidential participant contributions in GitHub feedback.

---

## Three things to remember

### Consent before capture

Being technically able to capture something does not mean it should be captured.

### Human review before authority

Transcripts, summaries and generated suggestions are drafts until people confirm them.

### Evidence before assertion

Important claims and outcomes should remain traceable to what actually happened in the session.
