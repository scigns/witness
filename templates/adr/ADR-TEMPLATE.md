## Context

What is the situation? What forces are at play? What makes this decision necessary *now*?

Write for someone reading this in 2036 who was not here. They will not have the background
conversation, the Slack thread, or the person who knew. State constraints explicitly, including
the non-technical ones — budget, staffing, politics, procurement. Those are usually the real
reasons, and omitting them makes the decision look arbitrary later.

## Compliance and enforcement

How is this decision enforced? A lint rule, a CI gate, a test, an architecture fitness function, a
code review checklist item — or nothing but goodwill (say so honestly if that is the case).
